## C-24 — N1: an ungated 六合 is LOCKED_FULL

**Ruling:** DECISIONS N1 (R-DEF-1), operator, 23 Sep 2026.
**Basis, located 原文:** 子平真詮 ch. 7 論刑沖會合解法, 「有戌，則卯與戌合而不沖」, with no 化 condition anywhere in the chapter (東里書齋, https://www.donglishuzhai.net/chapter/3720.html). Limiter in the same chapter, already carried by CORE-U6a: 「戌與寅午會，而卯復刑子」, where a 會 capturing the 合 partner revives the clash.
**What it corrects:** CORE 0.3.8 is the document the September audit (L-1) found in conflict with forensic practice. Its token grammar says an ungated 六合 produces LOCKED_PARTIAL; the forensic stack, now ruled, says LOCKED_FULL.

1. **Token grammar.** `LOCKED_FULL` = completed 三合, completed 三會, or **any** completed 六合, gate met or not. The transformation status travels separately, in Section 2: a gate-met 六合 is TRANSFORMED, an ungated one NEUTRALIZED. The sentence "A branch 六合 that fails the U6 gate produces LOCKED_PARTIAL on both branches; one that meets the gate produces LOCKED_FULL" is struck and replaced by: *"A branch 六合 produces LOCKED_FULL on both branches whether or not it meets the U6 gate; Section 2 states whether it is TRANSFORMED or NEUTRALIZED."*
2. **LOCKED_PARTIAL has no producer in CORE.** It stays in the grammar only so that a CHART-CONFIG generated before N1 can still be read. Such a CONFIG predates the ruling and must be regenerated by a forensic pass. Until it is, CORE does not reinterpret the token (that would re-derive a natal fact inside a daily run), and the existing LOCKED_PARTIAL limbs of CORE-U3, U4 and U5 apply to it unchanged. Those limbs are therefore **reachable only through a stale CONFIG**, and Section 0 states that the CONFIG predates N1.
3. **CORE-U6b.** For the two co-occurring pairs, 寅亥 and 巳申, an ungated 六合 now yields LOCKED_FULL, so the same-pair 破 reads **ELIMINATED** under CORE-U4, not ATTENUATED. The guard for 辰丑 and 子酉 is unchanged.
4. **[INTERIM · root via partial lock]** is renamed **[INTERIM · root via untransformed 六合 lock]** and keeps its behaviour: a branch locked by a 六合 whose U6 gate was not met keeps its hidden stems available for temporary root activation, exactly as a LOCKED_PARTIAL branch did in 0.3.8; a TRANSFORMED 六合 branch roots the new element only. **Held until FORENSIC-E-4** (hidden stem in a LOCKED_FULL branch as root) is ruled. 0.3.8 said nothing about roots through 三合 / 三會 locks, and this addendum adds nothing.

## C-25 — N2: a senior-layer standing clash no longer blocks an arriving combination

**Ruling:** DECISIONS N2 (R-DEF-2), operator, 23 Sep 2026: capture allowed across layers.
**Basis:** declared engine decision, inference tier. 子平真詮 ch. 25 論行運 原文 「又有逢沖而不沖者…而本命巳酉有相會，則沖無力；年支亥未，則卯逢年會而不沖月官」 (東里書齋, https://www.donglishuzhai.net/chapter/3738.html) describes an *arriving* branch whose own 會 beats its own clash; N2 applies it by analogy to a clash already standing when a later branch captures one leg (0.1f Addendum A §A5 makes the same distinction).

1. **CORE-U9 is replaced.** *"A branch engaged in an active standing 沖 may be captured by an arriving combination. The combination forms; the standing 沖 is then evaluated under CORE-U3 against the new token (LOCKED_FULL → ELIMINATED, stated in writing)."*
2. **Why no same-layer case arises in CORE.** Every CORE arrival (the monthly pillar at derivation step 2, the daily at step 3) is a different layer from the standing field it meets, so N2 always governs. The same-layer case (FORENSIC-E-8b, still escalated in the forensic resolver) can arise only inside CONFIG's natal field, which a forensic pass resolves and CORE only loads.
3. **BLOCKED's scope.** CORE-U9 was one of BLOCKED's two homes (0.3.5). It is removed. BLOCKED now has one home: CORE-U7, a stem combination whose partner is committed per CORE-U8. U7's definition loses its "standing clash per U9" limb.
4. **Cross-references updated:** CORE-U10 "(U6 gate checked; U9 applied first)" → "(U6 gate checked)"; Output Protocol "completions/re-derivations (U9→U10)" → "(U10)".
5. **Record note.** As DECISIONS N2 records, this reverses the September audit's L-7 correction to the 16 Sep daily addendum; that day's first headline (巳酉丑 completes and silences natal 卯酉) stands again as written.

## C-26 — N22: duplicate counting for 沖 and 子卯刑

**Ruling:** DECISIONS N22, operator, 23–24 Sep 2026: E-23 adopted on located 原文, scoped to 沖 and 子卯刑; transit scope option C; ch. 7 因解而反得刑沖 applied; follow-ups (a) and (b).
**Basis, located 原文:**
- ch. 7 (東里 3720): 「假如甲生子月，支逢二卯相並，二卯不刑一子」 and 「而支又逢戌，戌與卯合，本爲解刑，而合去其一，則一合而一刑，是因解而反得刑沖也」.
- ch. 25 (東里 3738), under 一沖而得兩沖: 「如乙用申官，兩申並而不沖一寅，運又逢寅，則運與本命，合成二寅，以沖二申之類是也」.
- 徐樂吾's note disputing the ch. 25 passage (評注 edition, per 0.1f Addendum A §A4) is recorded at commentary tier and does not override 原文.

**New rule CORE-U3a**, applying to 沖 and to the 子卯 刑 pair only. In CORE the natal layer is CONFIG's four pillars; the **transit layers are LP (decade), annual, monthly and daily.** 並 means present together in the field, not adjacent (ch. 25 pairs a luck branch with a natal one).

| Configuration (instances of the two branches) | State |
|---|---|
| One against one | per CORE-U3 / U5 as before |
| Two natal against one natal, all FREE | **NOT FIRING** (二卯不刑一子; 兩申並而不沖一寅) |
| Two natal against one natal plus one transit, all FREE | both pairs **FULL FORCE** (運與本命合成二寅，以沖二申) |
| Two against two, all natal, all FREE | both pairs **FULL FORCE** under CORE-U3; the E-23 carve-out covers the outnumbered shape only |
| Any other two-against-one with a transit in it (a transit supplies one of the outnumbering pair; a transit on the single side; both duplicates transit) | **UNRESOLVED — ESCALATE** (E-23-scope), candidates NOT FIRING / FULL FORCE |
| One against two duplicates, one duplicate locked | count FREE instances only: one free duplicate against a FREE single → **FULL FORCE** (因解而反得刑沖) |
| One against two duplicates, the single side locked | **ELIMINATED** per CORE-U3 / U5 |
| One against two duplicates, both duplicates locked | **ELIMINATED** |
| Every other count (three against one, two against two with a transit in any other shape, or with a lock) | **UNRESOLVED — ESCALATE** (E-23-scope) |

**Out of scope, escalated:** 害, 破, and the three-way 刑 sets (寅巳申, 丑戌未) with duplicate legs output UNRESOLVED — ESCALATE (E-23-scope). 自刑 is not touched by this correction.

**Section 2's closed state list gains NOT FIRING:** ELIMINATED / ATTENUATED / FULL FORCE / NOT FIRING / BLOCKED / NEUTRALIZED(grade) / TRANSFORMED / UNRESOLVED—ESCALATE. NOT FIRING is a result, named under the naming-precedes-absence clauses, never a silence.

## C-29 — conflict (1): one of two identical candidates combines

**Ruling:** the resolver governs. **Basis, located 原文:** 子平真詮 ch. 7 「支逢二卯相並…而支又逢戌，戌與卯合，本爲解刑，而合去其一」 — one 戌 combines one of two 卯.

CORE-U6c is replaced: where an arriving branch has two or more identical FREE candidates for the same combination, **the combination forms with one of them.** The seat follows FORENSIC-U10a's consumption order (E-3, a declared engine decision): natal Year → Month → Day → Hour, then LP, annual, monthly. It is flagged in writing: *"Arriving [branch] finds [n] identical FREE candidates for [combination]. Combination forms with [seat] per E-3 (declared); [others] remain FREE."* The unconsumed candidates stay FREE and are evaluated normally downstream, including under U3a. This supersedes C-14 and C-16, and [INTERIM · U6c multi-instance branch candidates] is retired.

## C-30 — conflict (2): the same-pair 破 inside 寅亥 / 巳申 escalates

**Ruling:** both documents escalate. The Section 2 derivation order ranks a combination against *third* branches; it says nothing about a combined pair's own 破. 0.3.4's claim that the order "resolves it" is struck, and so is C-24 item 3's ELIMINATED reading. The same-pair 破 inside a completed 寅亥 or 巳申 六合 outputs **UNRESOLVED — ESCALATE (FORENSIC-E-38)**, candidates ELIMINATED / FULL FORCE. 0.3.4 labelled this item "FORENSIC-E-24", which is a different register item; it is relabelled **E-38**, the pool-collision item. The 辰丑 / 子酉 guard is unchanged.

## C-31 — conflict (3): 午未 in a 午 or 未 month escalates (FORENSIC-E-14)

No located text. When the gate month is itself a leg of the 午未 combination, TRANSFORMED vs NEUTRALIZED is **UNRESOLVED — ESCALATE (FORENSIC-E-14)**. Under C-24 the lock is LOCKED_FULL either way. 0.3.8's sentence "completed Earth in a 未 month carries 燥" now applies only once the operator rules such a case transformed.

## C-32 — conflict (4): the branch 六合 gate carries both readings of 旺月

No located text. The branch 六合 gate carries the two readings the forensic resolver carries:
- **Seasonal:** 土 辰戌丑未 · 金 申酉 · 水 亥子 · 木 寅卯 · 火 巳午.
- **三合-month:** 金 巳酉丑 · 水 申子辰 · 木 亥卯未 · 火 寅午戌. Earth has no 三合 frame, so 土 reads 辰戌丑未 under both.

Where the two readings agree, the gate is MET or NOT MET. Where they disagree, UNRESOLVED — ESCALATE (U5-旺月). Where the natal month and the monthly layer's month give different answers, UNRESOLVED — ESCALATE (FORENSIC-E-5). This supersedes C-27's [INTERIM · branch 六合 gate]. Under C-24 the gate decides TRANSFORMED vs NEUTRALIZED only.

## C-33 — conflict (5): transit stem combinations escalate (FORENSIC-E-21)

No located text: 子平真詮 ch. 5's proximity mechanisms concern natal columns only, and transit 隔位 is unlocated. **Any combination in which a transit stem (LP, annual, monthly, daily) takes part outputs UNRESOLVED — ESCALATE (FORENSIC-E-21).** Candidates: COMBINED / HALF-COMBINED for a single claimant; 先合 (preemption) / 爭合 (mutual impairment) for several claimants.

- [INTERIM · monthly capture] is retired.
- **U8's clause "an adjacent arrival has capture priority over a latent partner" is struck.** It is the same unlocated transit-proximity question. The operator's ruling named only the monthly-capture default; this clause is struck under the same ruling, because leaving it would carry the conflict on in U8.
- [INTERIM · latent partner token] is retired with the capture rule it depended on.
- U8's COMMITTED-case identity closure and U8a's LATENT-case rule are unchanged.
- **Natal separated pairs are untouched.** C-33 reaches only combinations with a transit stem in them. A latent natal pair still loads from CONFIG, with its intervener, and reduces independence without committing; at the forensic layer that is ch. 5's 「隔位太遠…合而不能合也，半合也，其爲禍福得十之二三而已」 for the Year–Hour case, with a controlling intervener reducing it to 有若無. The only thing struck is the transit capture priority.

## C-34 — conflict (8): surplus-FREE for 自刑

**Basis, located:** 子平真詮 ch. 7 shows the freed 卯 acting in 刑 once the other is combined away (「而合去其一，則一合而一刑」); FORENSIC 0.1f CX-4 locates surplus-FREE for the 沖 / 刑 pools. For a 自刑 branch, count FREE instances: **two or more FREE → FULL FORCE**; fewer than two FREE → per CORE-U5 against the locked instance (LOCKED_FULL → ELIMINATED).
