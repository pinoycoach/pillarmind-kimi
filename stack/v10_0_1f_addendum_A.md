## §A3 — WHAT MERGING E-23 DOES TO RUNS ALREADY LOGGED

Ch. 25 原文: two 申 together do not clash a single 寅; when a luck pillar supplies a second 寅, both pairs clash (一沖而得兩沖). 徐樂吾 dissents in his note. If P-43 is adopted, two logged grades move:

- **AA.** The CRITICAL headline was the Day Master's sole root 寅 clashed by both 申 (Month and Day). Under ch. 25, two 申 do not clash one 寅, so **the natal CRITICAL does not hold.** But the current decade is 庚寅, which supplies the second 寅. Both pairs clash **for this decade**, so the CRITICAL returns for ages 54–63 only.
- **ces drilon.** The congenital 丑未 clash (one 丑 against 未 on Month and Day) is two-against-one, so **the natal clash does not fire.** It fires only where a transit supplies a second 丑.

Neither chart is re-run by this addendum. Both carry a note that their grade is contingent on P-43.

## §A4 — SECOND-EDITION CHECK ON CH. 25, DISCHARGED

0.1f Part Eight lists "ch. 25 on a second edition (CX-8)" as owed. Done: 子平真詮評注, ch. 25 論行運, 國學典籍網 edition, full chapter, with 原文 and 徐注 set apart — http://ab.newdu.com/book/ms201524.html

- **CX-8 confirmed on a second edition.** 「沖提綱月令為重，餘支為輕；沖喜用所在地為重」 is 徐注. It follows the 原文 sentence 「沖年月則急，沖日時則緩也」 and opens by disputing it (此說未可拘定).
- **運以方為重 located.** 0.1f is right that it is absent from ch. 27. Its actual home is ch. 25 徐注, together with 「看運須十年並論」. ZX's attribution was to the wrong chapter. Commentary tier.
- **The fivefold 取用 list** appears in the same 徐注 (扶抑、去病、通關、調候、助旺). A third confirmation that it is 徐's framework.
- **NEW for existing P-14.** Ch. 25 原文, 逢沖而不沖: a natal 巳酉 相會, two legs without 丑, makes a transit clash on 酉 powerless. An arriving 卯 that forms a 會 with the year branch does not clash the month. So a **half**-frame weakens a clash in 原文. V10 treats half-frames as tendencies with no effect on clashes. This is located text against that, and it goes to P-14.
- **Also for E-23:** the 兩申不沖一寅 passage and 徐's dissent sit in this same chapter.
