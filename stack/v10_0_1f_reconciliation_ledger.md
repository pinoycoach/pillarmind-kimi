## PART THREE — ITEMS EVERY PASS MISSED

**M-1 · E-19 has been framed on the wrong route.** Digong's transparent stems are 乙 (印) and 己己 (傷). The route those stems build in ch. 35 is **印而用傷食**, not 印遇財. Its predicate is stated at both poles:
- 「身强印旺，恐其太過，洩身以爲秀氣」 (ch. 35)
- 「若印淺身輕，而用層層傷食，則寒貧之局矣」 (ch. 35)
- 成 condition: 「身印兩旺而用食傷洩氣」 (ch. 9)
- 帶忌 condition: 「印透食神以洩其氣，而又遇財露」 (ch. 9)

Two further ch. 35 clauses may be live: 「印輕財重，又無劫財以救，則爲貪財破印」 and 「印重財輕，而兼露傷食，財與食相生…即可就富，亦不貴矣」. All turn on the same predicate. Ch. 36 adds that under 印用傷食 a 財 luck period is 反吉 when the 印 is adequate, and 「印輕者，亦不利見財」. **The sign of a 財 decade flips on E-19.** The 0.1e framing (印輕財重 as the central fork) should be checked for conflation of the two ch. 35 phrases.

**M-2 · Shen contradicts himself on the mixed 混雜 case** (ch. 31 李參政 vs ch. 25 / 27). Both-hidden is unaffected. → E-29.

**M-3 · 午's hidden stems** (ch. 10 vs ch. 27). U14 follows ch. 27 and the 三命通會 table. → E-30.

**M-4 · The branch half of the 5/5 split has a located five-year window** (ch. 28). → ZX-5 relabel.

**M-5 · 劫財 has two named jobs in 印格** (SuperGrok Q-4): rescue against 貪財破印, and 劫財以存煞印 (庚戌、戊子、甲戌、乙亥). P-1's route table must carry both.
