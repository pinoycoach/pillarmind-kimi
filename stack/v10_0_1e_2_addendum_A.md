## §B2 — DEFECT D-1: **R-2 IS REVERSED. CLAUDE'S MECHANISM IS THE LOCATED DOCTRINE.**

### The evidence 0.1e did not retrieve

0.1e closed E-1 and E-2 from a snippet containing the 庚間隔 example and the 兩辛合丙 passage. The full chapter, retrieved this pass, continues past both. Three clauses, in order:

**Clause A — 隔位太遠 (distance degrades):**

> 「又有隔位太遠，如甲在年干，己在時上，心雖相契，地則相遠，如人天南地北，不能相合一般。然於有所制而不敢合者，亦稍有差，**合而不能合也，半合也，其爲禍福得十之二三而已**。」

*Separated too far — 甲 on the Year stem, 己 on the Hour — hearts agree but places are distant. This differs from the case of a controlling intervener: it is a combination that cannot complete, a **half-combination**, delivering two or three tenths of its effect.*

Source: 東里書齋 — https://www.donglishuzhai.net/chapter/3718.html
Corroborated: https://www.douban.com/note/844705530/ and https://www.qire.net/book/436/c9b8ec27c8fb1.html

Distance is a real degrading mechanism, distinct from control, quantified at 十之二三. **0.1e's "the block is control, not distance" was a false dichotomy built on an incomplete retrieval.** Both mechanisms exist, with different severities: controlling intervener → 有若無 (as if absent); distance → 半合 (fractional).

**Clause B — 兩合一而隔位: the 高太尉 fixture:**

> 「**若以兩合一而隔位，則全無爭妒。**如庚午、乙酉、甲子、乙亥，兩乙合庚，甲日隔之，此高太尉命，仍作合煞留官，**無減福也**。」

*Where two combine one and one claimant is separated, **there is no contest at all**. 庚午 / 乙酉 / 甲子 / 乙亥: two 乙 reach for one 庚, with the Day 甲 standing between — Commander Gao's chart. The combination resolves cleanly, with no reduction of fortune.*

Source: same chapter, same URLs.

This is digong's configuration with the stems changed: two identical claimants, one partner, one claimant separated. **The chapter's own worked example says the contest does not arise.** The adjacent claimant combines cleanly; the separated claimant neither contests nor suffers (無減福). U13's mutual-impairment treatment — which 0.1e ratified for Gemini and DeepSeek — is the rule for **equal-standing claimants only** (兩辛合丙 with no separation). Digong's claimants are not equal-standing.

**Clause C — 本身之合 (the Day Master case, directly):**

> 「惟是本身十干合之，不爲合去…**若庚在年上，乙在月上，則月上之乙，先去合庚，而日干反不能合，是爲合去也**。又如女以官爲夫，丁日逢壬…惟壬在月上，而年丁合之，日干之丁，反不能合，**是以己之夫星，被姊妹合去，夫星透而不透矣**。」

*The Day Master's own combining never removes the Day Master. But where a rival stem sits closer to the partner, **the rival combines first and the Day Master cannot** — the star is combined away. As a woman whose husband star is combined away by a sister: transparent, yet as if not transparent.*

Source: same chapter; corroborated at https://www.suanzhun.net/book/317.html

The passage answers the exact question 0.1e escalated in its Item 1: does the Day Master's position give it standing regardless of distance? **No. The closer rival preempts, and the Day Master 反不能合.**
