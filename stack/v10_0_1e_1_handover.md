## R-2. THE 丙 / 丙 / 辛 CONTEST — **GEMINI AND DEEPSEEK GOVERN**

**Contested:** Gemini and DeepSeek both ruled CONTESTED under U13, mutual impairment, no clean capture, GRADE tier. Claude resolved it by extending the restored Latent Combination Rule to stems by analogy: proximity decides, Annual 丙 captures 辛, the Day Master's 丙 stands latent.

**Ruling: Gemini and DeepSeek. Two independent clauses of one chapter close it.**

### Clause one: the intervention question

子平真詮 ch. 5 論十干合而不合 states the blocking mechanism precisely:

> 「然而亦有合而不合者，何也？**蓋隔於有所間也**…譬如甲與己合，而甲己中間，以庚間隔之，**則甲豈能越克我之庚而合己？此制於勢然也**。」

*There are combinations that do not combine. Why? Because they are separated by something standing between. Suppose 甲 would combine with 己, and 庚 stands between them: how could 甲 reach past the 庚 that controls it, to combine with 己? This is the constraint of position.*

Source: 算準網 子平真詮 index — https://www.suanzhun.net/dianji/zipingzhen/
Chapter position corroborated: https://www.donglishuzhai.net/chapter/3719.html (chapter listing, 五、論十干合而不合)

**The stated blocking condition is not distance. It is an intervening stem that controls (克) the combining stem.** 甲 cannot reach past 庚 because 庚 剋 甲.

Apply it. Day 丙 and Decade 辛 are separated by Hour 己. Does 己 control 丙? No. 丙 生 己. The intervener is the Day Master's own output, not its controller. **Per the stated mechanism, the intervention does not block.** The Day Master's 丙 retains standing to combine with 辛.

This directly narrows E-2 and it narrows it against Claude. The 隔合 doctrine as actually written does not degrade-by-distance; it blocks-by-control. A non-controlling intervener leaves the combination live.

### Clause two: two claimants, one partner

Same chapter, the passage already located in the 5 August handover as F4:

> 「然又有爭合妬合之說，何也？**如兩辛合丙，兩丁合壬之類**，一夫不娶二妻，一女不配二夫，所以有爭合妬合之說。**然到底終有合意，但情不專耳。**」

*Two 辛 combining with one 丙, or two 丁 with one 壬: one husband does not marry two wives, one woman is not matched to two husbands. In the end there is still combining intent; only the affection is not exclusive.*

The example is structurally identical to digong's, inverted (two of one stem, one partner). Both 丙 have standing. Neither takes exclusive possession. **合意 survives; 情不專.** That is mutual impairment, stated in the source, not a conservative engine setting.

### Why Claude loses on the spec's own terms

0.1d Patch 1 labels the Latent Combination Rule *"an inherited engine rule with a stated V9 provenance"* with no locator, and §9 lists it among declared engine decisions, each of which is *"superseded immediately by a page-verified citation, in either direction."* The citation has landed. Claude's extension of an unlocated rule, by analogy, from branches to stems, is superseded twice: once on the intervention mechanism, once on the contest outcome.

**E-2 moves NARROWED → CLOSED. E-1 moves OPEN → CLOSED.** Both by ch. 5, one opening, exactly as the 5 August handover predicted.
