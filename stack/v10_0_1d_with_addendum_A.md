## WHAT THIS DOCUMENT IS

Every prior V10 document is a **patch ledger** — it records changes made to V10 by V10, arising from V10's own runs. This one is different in kind. It is a **reconciliation ledger**: it audits the transition from V9 Final to V10 Draft 0.1, which was never diffed.

The audit was prompted by a single observation. V10's U7 second clause — struck under 0.1a Patch 3 as admitting three incompatible readings — is a **transcription inversion** of V9 Final Standing Rule 26, which is unambiguous. That raised the question of whether the inversion was isolated. It was not.

**Method.** Every Standing Rule, scratchpad Step, Stage, Mode, Mode B section, Failure Mode, and reference table in V9 Final was assigned exactly one disposition against V10 0.1c: CARRIED, SUPERSEDED, DROPPED, INVERTED, or MISSED. Part One is that table. Part Two is the patches arising. Part Three revisits F12, which the audit unblocks.

**What this document is not.** It contains no new classical claims, no located citations, and no instrument adjudication. Every finding is a text comparison between two documents already in the corpus. That is deliberate: the failure being repaired was caused by *not* doing the text comparison, and it cannot be repaired by another round of the thing that missed it.

**Certification impact.** 0.1d does not advance the certification path. It changes what the fixture suite must be built against. Fixtures designed before this reconciliation would have certified a specification with no hidden-stem sourcing rule and no special-formation gate — passing charts it should fail, undetectably.

## PATCH 1 — RESTORE THE LATENT COMBINATION RULE
### Location: §2.2 — U7, second clause
### Type: Restoration of an inverted rule. Reverses 0.1a Patch 3
### Importance: Severity 1
### Disposition: CLOSED by text comparison. No instrument pass required

### Why

V9 Final states the rule twice, in two places, identically in direction.

**Standing Rule 26:**
> Latent Combination Rule. Natal stems separated by intervening stems form latent combinations. **Adjacent arriving stems have full capture priority.**

**Reference tables, Latent Combination Rule (attributed to 子平真詮 proximity principles):**
> When an arriving stem has closer proximity to one of the latent partners, **the arriving stem has full capture priority.** The natal latent partner that was not captured stands freer than full commitment would suggest.

V10 U7's second clause, as it stood before 0.1a:
> "Latent partners have capture priority for adjacent arrivals."

**These are opposite rules.** V9 gives priority to the *arriving* stem. V10 gave it to the *latent partner*. The V10 sentence is not an ambiguous rendering of V9's — it is a reversal, and its incoherence is what generated the three parses 0.1a Patch 3 correctly identified.

0.1a's remedy was removal, reasoning that "reinterpreting it would require ruling F12, which is suspended." That reasoning is sound if the V10 sentence is the only source. It is not the source. V9 Final is, and V9 Final is unambiguous in both statements. **The rule did not need interpreting. It needed retrieving.**

Net effect of 0.1a Patch 3 as it stands: V10 has no latent-combination priority rule at all, where V9 had a clear one stated twice.

### Amendment to U7

> **Strike** the [0.1a] clause routing latent-partner contests to U13 as the primary treatment.
>
> **Restore, from V9 Final SR26 and the V9 Final reference tables:**
>
> > **Latent Combination Rule.** Natal stems separated by an intervening stem form a **latent** combination, not a committed one. A latent combination reduces the independence of both stems, commits neither to permanent fusion, and can be displaced by an arriving annual or monthly stem with closer proximity. **Where an arriving stem has closer proximity to one of the latent partners, the arriving stem has full capture priority.** The latent partner that was not captured stands freer than full commitment would suggest — but not at full independence.
>
> **U13 relationship.** U13 governs contests between stems of **equal proximity**. Where proximity differs, the Latent Combination Rule resolves and U13 does not fire. Where proximity is equal or indeterminable, U13 fires as written.

### Provenance and its limit

V9 Final attributes this rule to "子平真詮 proximity principles" with no locator. **Under E20 it therefore does not enter V10 as classical authority.** It enters as an **inherited engine rule** with a stated V9 provenance — the same tier as R19 and U10a's consumption order, and it must be labelled in §9's declared-engine-decisions list accordingly.

This is a real constraint and it is not a formality. The rule is being restored because it is what the instrument has been running on since V9, stated consistently in two places, and because losing it left a hole. It is not being restored because the doctrine was verified. It was not.

### Effect

- V10 regains a latent-combination priority rule.
- E-2 (隔合 standing) narrows substantially: V9's rule *is* a 隔合 doctrine, and it holds that separation produces latency rather than disqualification — which is the position both instruments reached independently in 0.1c ("separation degrades; nobody established disqualification"). E-2 moves from OPEN to **NARROWED**; the residue is whether a latent partner retains standing to *contest* as opposed to being *displaced*.
- F12 unblocks. See Part Three.

## PATCH 2 — HIDDEN STEM SOURCING
### Location: §2.2 — new rule U14
### Type: Restoration of a MISSED rule
### Importance: Severity 1

### Why

V9 SR2: *"Hidden stem table is absolute. 三命通會 lookup only. Never derive. If the table does not list that element for that branch, it does not exist in that branch."* V9 Final carries the twelve-branch table and states beneath it that every attribution must trace to a specific entry and that elemental inference does not override it.

### Also add: §2.6, the Hidden Stem Table

## PATCH 5 — THE INSTRUMENT INTEGRITY LAYER
### Location: §1 pipeline, and new Appendix B
### Type: Restoration of Stage 2 and the Failure Mode catalogue
### Importance: Severity 1 (catalogue), Severity 2 (audit step)

### Why

V10 has an Escalation Register, which tracks **what the classical texts do not settle**. V9 had a Failure Mode catalogue, which tracked **what instruments reliably get wrong**. These are different artifacts answering different questions, with different remedies — a register item needs a citation, a failure mode needs an enforcement mechanism. V10 kept one and lost the other.

The catalogue is not abstract. Categories A through G each carry a documented instance with a reproduction count, and several were reproduced by *multiple independent instruments on the same chart*. Category B in particular has a live entry — the Opus 4.8 void re-derivation of June 2026, which produced confident first-principles arithmetic that inverted a sexagenary index and would have rewritten the corpus had the Tier 2 check not caught it. That entry is the single most instructive artifact in the V9 corpus and it is currently in no V10 document.

Relatedly, V9's Stage 2 Translation Audit was a **step**: before writing, trace every elemental claim to a scratchpad line, and where the scratchpad contradicts the claim, the scratchpad wins. V10 §2.5 states the standard but places no audit between the Resolved Field Map and the prose. Failure Mode A is precisely the failure of drifting from a correct map, and it is caught by running the audit, not by having asserted that unsupported statements are unsupported.

### Addition to §1, as a pipeline step between 4 and 5

> **4a. Translation Audit** — before the Shield runs, trace every elemental and structural claim written in §5 to its line in the Resolved Field Map. Where the map does not support a claim, strike it. Where the map contradicts a claim, **the map governs**; rewrite to match.
>
> Three checks, run explicitly:
> 1. No sentence references data format, source, or instrument structure.
> 2. Every scarcity or absence claim traces to the element inventory, with decade and transit surface stems counted at full 透出 weight.
> 3. Every eliminated interaction is absent from the prose; every claimed interaction has a map line.

### New Appendix B — Documented Failure Modes

> Carry V9 Final's Categories A–G verbatim, with instances and reproduction counts. Add:
>
> **Category H — Undiffed inheritance (V10 0.1d).** A generation change carried forward as a rewrite rather than a mapping. Twenty-one rules were lost or inverted between V9 Final and V10 Draft 0.1, and four adjudication rounds on the new document did not surface any of them, because each round tested the new document's internal coherence rather than its inheritance. *Prevention: the transition rule in Patch 8.*
>
> Appendix B is maintained on the same discipline as the Escalation Register: an entry is added when a failure is observed, and entries are not retired without a stated reason.

### Also restore, to §8 Mode A

> **Jailbreak Frame Check.** Before delivering Mode A prose: *"Would the Mount Hua hermit speaking only from 渊海子平 and 滴天髓 say this?"* If no, rewrite. The two-page boundary is the symptom check; this is the cause check. Both run.

## PATCH 7 — PROSE DISCIPLINE
### Location: §0 System Instruction
### Type: Consolidation of four MISSED rules
### Importance: Severity 3

### Why

Four V9 Standing Rules governed what the instrument may *say*, as distinct from what it may conclude: SR11 (structural vocabulary), SR14 (no behavioural prescriptions, with a banned-phrase list and the Mechanical Necessity substitution), SR15 (conditions not outcomes, general), SR16 (commercial reframing blocked).

### Addition to §0

> **Prose discipline — four standing prohibitions.**
>
> 1. **Vocabulary is structural.** No luck, fate, destiny, fortune, auspicious, inauspicious.
